# BOOM! website content workshop

The live site gives us enough to build the core story, service pages, values, team profiles and working approach. A few elements that would make the new site more persuasive need BOOM-approved evidence or decisions before they can be published.

## Work and client proof

The current site lists case studies as a communication service, but I could not find published project stories. For each of three to six launch case studies, agree:

- The challenge and therapy area
- The audience and market
- BOOM's role, services and deliverables
- The result, with any supporting evidence
- Whether the story can name the client or must be anonymised
- Which creative assets can be shown

Also identify approved client quotes and logos, including the name, role and wording that each client has cleared for publication. The proposed new site should add a Work page and selected-work placements on Home and What we do once this material is ready.

## Veeva credentials

The live How we do it page lists Veeva PromoMats approval processes and Veeva sales aid and approved email content work. It does not substantiate Veeva Multichannel or Vault Content Partner status, or list certifications. Confirm the official partner title, scope and current certificates before adding a badge.

## Careers

The public material supports the people-first message and remote and face-to-face ways of working. Confirm what can be said about current vacancies, hybrid or office expectations, benefits, professional development, and the preferred careers contact. No careers@ address or current opening was found on the public pages reviewed.

## Contact and locations

The public contact page names London, Bournemouth and Copenhagen, lists Cory and Viv's business emails and mobile numbers, but gives no street addresses or general enquiries email. Confirm:

- The approved London, Bournemouth and Copenhagen address details and map links
- Whether personal mobile numbers should stay public
- A general enquiries email and a careers contact
- Where form submissions should go and who should access them

The Terms page lists 1124 Christchurch Road as the registered office and 99 Bargates as the main trading address. The Privacy page lists a different registered-office address. Confirm the correct details before adding them to a new site.

## Brand, imagery and digital delivery

- Supply the approved logo pack, including an SVG wordmark and favicon. The concept currently uses the logo image linked from BOOM's public site.
- Approve team headshots for reuse. The concept links to the public Squarespace image files and should use supplied, self-hosted assets in production.
- The pigment burst and process photograph in the concept are AI-generated illustrations. Approve, replace or remove them before external publication.
- Confirm the production domain and URL structure before adding canonical URLs and a sitemap.
- Confirm analytics, cookie use, form data handling and consent requirements before launch. The legal text and privacy notice should be reviewed for the new site and any new form fields.
- Choose the CMS and agree who will add case studies, vacancies and news after launch.


## Repositioning ideas and claims to confirm

The proposed copy in the repositioning brief is a creative direction, not automatically verified source material. Use the right-band idea as the working brand story, while confirming the following details with BOOM before publication:

- Whether BOOM wants to publicly tell the Traveling Wilburys hiring story and use “Wilbury” as a staff identity. Keep the team and careers copy people-led without it until approved.
- The current team size and how it is counted. Do not use “65 specialists” until confirmed.
- Veeva partner titles, products and current certifications. The public site supports PromoMats approval-process and approved-content capabilities, not partner status.
- Any 2026 workplace award, badge or recognition, plus the exact award name and permission to display it.
- Career details such as 8.45 daily calls, no timesheets, hybrid expectations, holiday allowance, benefits and pension. These need a current job advert or direct confirmation.
- Any unapproved client outcomes, quotes, logos, specialist-lead assignments or personal stories. Case-study examples in the brief are explicitly illustrative and must not be presented as BOOM work.
- Whether to promise a one-working-day response, and the right careers and general enquiry email addresses.

The draft uses the user-supplied repositioning as editorial guidance, live-site material for public facts, and labels workshop questions where claims need confirmation.
